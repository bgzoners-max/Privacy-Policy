Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

Private Planner ("we", "our", or "the app") is designed with a strong focus on privacy. This app works offline and does not require account creation.

2. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • No account registration
  • No personal information collected
  • No analytics tracking
  • No third-party tracking services

3. Local Storage

Private Planner stores your data locally on your device (e.g., IndexedDB or local database).

  • Tasks
  • Notes
  • Planner entries

This data is not transmitted to any server, not accessible by us, and fully controlled by you.

4. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally:

  • You are responsible for your device security
  • We do not have access to your data
  • Deleting the app may delete all stored data

5. Third-Party Services

Private Planner does not use third-party services that collect user data.

6. Children’s Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any data from children or any users.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date.
